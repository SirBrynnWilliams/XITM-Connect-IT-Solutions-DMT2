Core facility SOPs

Five concise operating procedures supporting reliable, secure facility services.

SOP 01

Core software and REDCap binding

Maintain approved research software through change control. The REDCap application service is hosted at 156.10.10.12; validate access and backups after approved maintenance. Handle research data only in authorised systems.

SOP 02

RSTP loop prevention

Keep Rapid Spanning Tree Protocol enabled on managed campus switches. Changes to trunks, root bridge priority or switch topology require an approved change and post-change loop/connectivity checks.

SOP 03

Power contingency and load-shedding

Monitor the 42U MDF rack smart UPS, battery alerts and diesel generator readiness. During load-shedding, confirm protected systems remain on backup power and follow controlled shutdown/escalation if runtime approaches safe limits.

SOP 04

Centralised DHCP allocation

Use central DHCP scopes for managed devices and VLANs. Reserve addresses through the approved process; do not assign unapproved static addresses. Verify lease, gateway and DNS during troubleshooting.

SOP 05

Local failover and disaster recovery

Declare an incident, protect systems and data, and use documented failover procedures. Use approved offline LIMS caching during connectivity loss, restore from verified backups, validate service integrity and record recovery actions before returning to normal operations.
