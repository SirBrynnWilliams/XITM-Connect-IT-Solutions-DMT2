IT hardware acquisition workflow

A transparent seven-stage path from an identified need to a recorded, supported asset.

  1. 01

    Requirement

    Document the operational need, users affected, compatibility, security and lifecycle requirements.

  2. 02

    Request

    Submit a purchase request with business justification, specifications and budget owner.

  3. 03

    Evaluation

    IT reviews technical fit, supportability, security, total cost and supplier options.

  4. 04

    CIO approval

    The CIO/IT Manager checks priority, budget and policy compliance before authorisation.

  5. 05

    Procurement

    Procurement places the approved order and retains the required quotation and purchase records.

  6. 06

    Deployment

    IT receives, configures, secures and tests the equipment before handover to the assigned user.

  7. 07

    Asset logging

    Record asset tag, model, serial number, assigned location, custodian and support lifecycle.
