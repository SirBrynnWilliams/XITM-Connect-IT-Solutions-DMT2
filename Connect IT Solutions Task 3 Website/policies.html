IT security and acceptable use

Practical baseline rules for protecting facility systems, people and information.

Passwords and MFA

  • Use a minimum of 12 characters with a unique passphrase.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on supported accounts.
  • Never share credentials; report suspected compromise immediately.
  • Lock unattended workstations and use only approved account recovery.

Cabling and physical safety

  • Use approved LSZH fire-retardant Cat6a cabling and certified installation methods.
  • Do not obstruct routes, exits or equipment ventilation with cables.
  • Only authorised staff may access MDF/IDF rooms or alter network cabling.
  • Report damaged cables, exposed wiring or hazards to the service desk.

Acceptable use

  • Lenovo ThinkCentre PCs and HP LaserJet printers are for approved facility work.
  • Install software only with IT approval; do not connect rogue switches or access points.
  • Keep research information on approved systems; no personal cloud or unencrypted USB copies.
  • Use printers responsibly and collect confidential documents promptly.

Data protection

  • Access only information required for your assigned duties.
  • Use approved transfer and storage tools for sensitive research information.
  • Report accidental disclosure or loss promptly through the incident process.
  • Public pages must never disclose identifying research or patient information.

These project guidelines are illustrative and should be reviewed against approved facility policies and applicable law before operational use.
