Internal · IT operations

IT service roles

Roles, responsibilities and service escalation for Lumko, supported by Connect IT Solutions.

Leadership

CIO / IT Manager

Owns IT strategy, budgets, procurement approvals, governance and operational performance. Reports to the Facility Director.

Infrastructure

Senior Network Engineer

Maintains routing, VLANs, RSTP, campus switching, Wi-Fi and the OM3 fibre backbone.

Platforms

Systems Administrator

Maintains server platforms, identity, SQL services, backups, REDCap and LIMS availability.

Assurance

Cybersecurity / POPIA Officer

Coordinates security controls, access reviews, privacy practices, incident response and audit evidence.

User support · 8 technicians

Tier 1 / Tier 2 Service Desk Technicians

Two technicians are based in the Main Block and two in each of the three laboratory buildings. They log and triage incidents, resolve common device issues and escalate network, systems or safety events.
