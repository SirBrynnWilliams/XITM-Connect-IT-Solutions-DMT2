Our story

Growing a research institute to meet the needs of its people and its science.

The name Lumko reflects wisdom: the understanding gained through learning, study and careful observation of human health. Established in 2018, Lumko Medical Research Institute began as a small private research practice with a clear purpose: to support medical knowledge through careful study of the human body. As our work and team grew, it became clear that we needed more room for people, specialist equipment and the technology that modern research depends on.

We moved into a larger, repurposed facility in Walmer, Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth), where our administration and laboratory teams can work together across a four-building campus. The institute supports research and diagnostic services, including laboratory screening and access to MRI imaging pathways, subject to clinical assessment, availability and referral requirements.

The move also revealed that the existing network, servers and workstations could not reliably support the institute's growing needs. Lumko partnered with Connect IT Solutions, our IT services provider, to modernise the network infrastructure and strengthen connectivity across the campus. Laboratory information systems, blood analysers, tissue imaging equipment and digital microscopes all rely on dependable, well-managed technology.

Today, we continue to bring research, diagnostics and thoughtful technology together. We are committed to responsible research practice, respectful communication and collaboration with the communities and academic partners we serve.
