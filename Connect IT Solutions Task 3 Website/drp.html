Contingency response

Declare and coordinate incidents through the authorised response leads.

SQL service failure

Notify systems administration, protect logs and storage, assess replica/backup integrity, restore to a verified point and validate dependent applications.

Load-shedding

Monitor UPS capacity, preserve critical services and perform controlled shutdowns if safe runtime cannot be maintained.

Fibre severance

Isolate the affected link, report physical hazards, use approved alternate paths where available and coordinate repair and connectivity checks.

Ransomware

Isolate affected devices, alert security leadership, preserve evidence and restore only from verified clean backups after containment.

Fire or water hazard

Raise the facility alarm and follow evacuation and safety procedures. Do not enter an affected server room; contact emergency responders and facilities management, then assess equipment and recovery options when the area is declared safe.
