Facility Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Summary key points of core operational protocols governing the Lumko Medical Research Institute:

SOP 01: Core Software Management

Governs REDCap and LIMS application server bindings (156.10.10.12), user role permissions, and POPIA privacy compliance.

SOP 02: Information Security & Access Control

Mandates 12+ character complex passwords, Active Directory MFA, and LSZH fire-retardant network cabling standards.

SOP 03: Acceptable Use of IT Infrastructure

Restricts Lenovo ThinkCentre workstations to official facility functions and prohibits unauthorized software installations.

SOP 04: IT Hardware Procurement & Acquisition

Defines formal approval workflows for acquiring hardware, logging equipment assets, and managing HP LaserJet leasing contracts.

SOP 05: IT Helpdesk & Incident Escalation

Outlines Tier 1/2 triage procedures, issue reporting workflows, and response SLAs for network, power, and workstation failures.

Network & IT

Report connectivity, Wi-Fi, wired ports, workstations, or a network service. Include the building/floor and symptoms; do not include account passwords.

Research & Equipment

Report an equipment workflow, availability, run, or maintenance concern. Include an approved asset tag if available; leave out patient, participant, and study identifiers.

An equipment issue may involve both areas: report operational symptoms in Research & Equipment and network symptoms in Network & IT. A qualified biomedical or vendor contact should confirm a device’s approved network connection.
